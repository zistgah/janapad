# CONTRACT overlay --- Humanesque Mahajanapad, Volume III

Thin overlay on the master contract at `zistgah/governance`. It ADDS; it
never relaxes.

1.  **The plates are canonical.** A defect found later becomes a NEW
    plate and a new version, never an edit to a published one.
2.  **Preserve the hedging.** Panels marked hypothesis, analogy,
    conjecture or correlation are reproduced as such. A summary that
    promotes either to an established finding is a contract violation,
    not a tidy-up.
3.  **Preserve the primitives.** Inclination ≠ gradient ≠ probability ≠
    intent ≠ Act ≠ execution. Configuration-time ≠ physical time by
    assertion. PEDLER configuration-space constructs ≠ established
    spacetime physics. Collapsing any of these distinctions in derived
    text or metadata is ontological substitution.
4.  **No vendor name in any text layer** --- titles, slugs, config,
    metadata, overlays.
5.  **`assets/figures` is data.** Only declared figures plus the cover
    may live there; the seeder's contract check refuses anything
    undeclared, because an undeclared file WILL be minted.
6.  **Nothing pushes or mints without his typed gate.** An AI writes the
    script; he runs it.
7.  **The tool's interface is read, never recalled** --- and a
    discovered verb is not a discovered signature
    (`misty ots stamp <path>`, one path, no globs).
8.  **No fabricated bibliographic identifiers.** ISBNs, DOIs,
    publication dates, edition numbers and other identifiers must not be
    invented. Use only identifiers actually supplied or independently
    verified.
9.  **The Humanesque Mahajanapad title is canonical for this volume.**
    Do not silently revert the volume title to "The Dukedom of
    Humanesque."
10. **Preserve the distinction between historical analogy and historical
    identity.** Janapada/Mahajanapada and ancient gana-sangha traditions
    may inform the conceptual framing, but they must not be represented
    as identical to modern universal democracy.
